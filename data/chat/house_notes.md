# House notes

These rules sit in every answer. Edit this file to change them. No code change is required.

- Chattarpur, GK1 Cloud Kitchen, and Faridabad Sec-15 (closed from 23 Oct 2026) are out of the sales base.
- Rosedale Plaza was partly open and closed from 10 Nov 2026. A dip there is not unexplained softness.
- Manisquare and Forum in-store bills include mall bulk entries. Keep those bills, and the average per bill, out of comparisons.
- NCR dips in Navratri weeks are seasonal.
- Delhi and NCR recipe costs run higher than Kolkata on purpose (3 chutneys versus 1). Compare each city only with its own baseline.
- Food cost is raw consumption plus semi-processed consumption, divided by Posist gross. Packaging and other consumption are separate. Raw-only is not food cost. A store result under 15% or over 45% of gross looks off and is not stated as fact. Faridabad stays out of food-cost comparisons.
- Swiggy and Zomato charge 21% commission plus 18% GST on that commission, on the net value of each bill. That is 24.78% of net. Do not apply it to dine-in or takeaway. If a payout or ads file is loaded, use that actual and show the gap versus 24.78%. Channel files that are gross are not net.
- Stores missing from Reelo are intentional. Do not flag them.
