# Keka aggregates as of 12 Nov 2026

November counts cover 1–12 Nov only. Monthly joiner and exit counts of 0 are real counts.

These files are counts only. They do not include names or employee numbers.

- Location on a joiner is the current or last Keka location. There is no location history, so a transfer is counted at the current store.
- Entity for people who have left was inferred when the export had no legal entity.
- One person on notice had a last working day of 12 Nov 2026 and was still on the notice export, so they stay on notice.
- `match_status` is `matched` only when the Keka location maps to a Posist store on the store master. Unmatched locations stay unmatched.
